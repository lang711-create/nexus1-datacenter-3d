1. How the Power Shell Plugs into the Facility & Busbar

Overhead 415V 3-Phase Wye (240V Line-to-Neutral) track busways feature twist-in tap-off boxes above every cabinet. Heavy-gauge flexible power whips drop into the rear of the rack using IEC 60309 60A/63A Pin-and-Sleeve watertight connectors (or Anderson Saf-D-Grid plugs). Inside the 1OU ORV3 33kW Power Shelf, six hot-swappable 5,500W PSU blades rectify 415V AC into regulated 54V DC (674A per shelf). The rear of the power shelf features a floating high-current busbar clamp that bolts or blind-mates directly onto the vertical rack busbar.

2. How Compute Sleds Plug In (3-Way Blind-Mate)

When an engineer slides a 1OU GB200 compute tray into the rack, zero hand cables are plugged in at the rear. Three interfaces mate simultaneously via guide pins:
• Electrical: An Amphenol BarKlip BK500 / TE Crown Clip floating connector clamps onto the laminated 54V DC busbar (+54V and Return bars).
• Hydraulic (DLC): Dual Stäubli/Parker UQDB (Universal Quick Disconnect Blind-Mate) spring-loaded valves mate with the vertical water manifolds with zero fluid spill.
• Data Spine: Rear Paladin/ExaMAX high-speed backplane cartridges mate with the 5,184 twinax copper NVLink 5 spine.

3. Transient Load Smoothing (BBU + Supercap Shelf)

Synchronous LLM training causes all 72 GPUs to ramp from 20kW idle to 132kW full load in milliseconds—creating violent di/dt current spikes and grid flicker. Modern GB200/GB300 racks integrate Lithium-Ion Capacitor (LIC) Supercapacitor & BBU shelves directly on the 54V DC busbar. Providing 65 Joules per GPU of fast electrostatic storage, these shelves absorb millisecond transients and shave peak AC grid demand by up to 30%.
